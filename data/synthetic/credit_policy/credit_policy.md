# Commercial Term Lending Credit Policy (fictional)

Policy version CP-2026.2. This policy belongs to a fictional bank and exists only to test the axiom_agent_platform. It is not guidance for any real lender.

## CP-1 Purpose and scope

CP-1.1 This policy governs commercial term loans from $250,000 to $5,000,000 to operating businesses with at least two years of operating history.

CP-1.2 It sets the minimum financial standards, the documents required, and the escalation path for exceptions. It does not cover real estate, construction, or consumer lending.

## CP-2 Roles and decisions

CP-2.1 Relationship managers prepare and submit credit requests. They may not approve them.

CP-2.2 Credit officers approve, decline, or return requests for revision. A credit officer may not approve a request that the same person submitted.

CP-2.3 Automated tools may prepare analysis and draft memos. Every credit decision is made by an authorized credit officer.

## CP-3 Required documents

CP-3.1 Three fiscal years of income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements. A request with fewer than three years must be flagged and may proceed only with an explanation of the missing period.

CP-3.2 A signed loan application stating amount, rate, term, amortization, and purpose.

CP-3.3 A business description covering ownership, operating history, and customer concentration.

CP-3.4 Figures must reconcile: EBITDA must equal revenue less cost of goods sold and operating expenses, and total assets must equal total liabilities plus equity. Unreconciled statements are returned to the relationship manager before analysis continues.

## CP-4 Financial standards

CP-4.1 **Debt service coverage.** Pro forma DSCR, measured as latest-year EBITDA divided by existing interest, existing scheduled principal, and the proposed loan's annual payments, must be at least 1.25x.

CP-4.2 **Leverage.** Pro forma leverage, measured as total debt including the proposed loan divided by latest-year EBITDA, must not exceed 3.50x. Requests with zero or negative EBITDA do not meet this standard.

CP-4.3 **Liquidity.** The current ratio must be at least 1.20x in the latest fiscal year.

CP-4.4 **Interest coverage.** EBIT divided by interest expense must be at least 2.00x in the latest fiscal year.

CP-4.5 **Revenue trend.** A year-over-year revenue decline of more than 10% must be flagged and explained.

## CP-5 Concentration

CP-5.1 Revenue concentration above 25% in a single customer must be identified in the memo, with the contract terms and the effect on cash flow if that customer is lost.

CP-5.2 Concentration above 40% requires a sensitivity analysis and is normally approved only with conditions.

## CP-6 Exceptions

CP-6.1 A request that fails any CP-4 standard may be approved only as an exception. The memo must name each failed standard, the mitigating factors, and any conditions.

CP-6.2 Exceptions are approved by a credit officer and reported monthly to the credit committee.

CP-6.3 A request whose pro forma DSCR is below 1.00x is not eligible for an exception: the borrower's cash flow does not cover its debt service. It is declined.

## CP-7 Fair lending

CP-7.1 Credit decisions must rest on the borrower's financial condition and the terms of the request. The owner's name, race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, or age must not affect the analysis or the recommendation.
